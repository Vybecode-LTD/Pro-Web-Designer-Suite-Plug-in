What a ten-year guarantee actually costs
 

We repaired 1,412 jackets last year. That is up from 890 the year before, which sounds like a quality problem and is actually the opposite — the guarantee is eleven years old now, and the jackets from 2015 are finally wearing through at the cuffs.

The number people ask for is the cost. It was $61.40 a jacket, all in: thread, fabric, two repair technicians, and the return postage both ways. Against an average order of $248, that is a shade under 25% of revenue on any jacket that eventually comes back, and about 4% across everything we sell.
A guarantee that never gets used is not generous. It is a claim nobody tested.

Why we publish this

Because the number is the argument. Anyone can print "lifetime guarantee" on a hangtag. Very few will tell you what they spent honouring it, and the ones that will not are usually the ones hoping you never ask. The full breakdown, with the per-repair costs, is on the site.

Marisol, who has re-cuffed more Ridgelines than anyone alive.
